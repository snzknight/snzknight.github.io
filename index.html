01 / Profile

About

I am a master's graduate from the College of Electronic Engineering, Faculty of Information Science and Engineering, Ocean University of China (OUC), where I was fortunate to be supervised by Prof. Haiyong Zheng.

My graduate research focuses on interactive applications of AI large models, exploring natural and efficient human–computer interaction approaches that strengthen models' perceptual and responsive capabilities to deploy them into practical real-world settings. More broadly, my research interests encompass deep learning and computer vision.
