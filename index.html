01 — Profile

About

I am a master's graduate from the College of Electronic Engineering, Ocean University of China. I was supervised by Prof. Haiyong Zheng.

My graduate research focused on interactive applications of AI large models. I explored natural and efficient human–computer interaction approaches that strengthen models' perceptual and responsive capabilities and help bring them into practical settings. More broadly, my academic interests include deep learning and computer vision.
